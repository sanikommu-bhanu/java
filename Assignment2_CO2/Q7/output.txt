---- NOTES / ANSWERS ----
Chosen: Q6 triage with the fair (aging) rule. DIAGNOSIS: effective priority depends on 'now', so the naive version recomputes it for every waiting patient on every next() = O(n) per call, O(n^2) overall (800 million evaluations for 40,000 patients). OBJECTIVE: same order, next() in O(log n).
REDESIGN: effective = severity*W + arrival - now; 'now' is common to all patients, so ordering by arrival + severity*W gives the identical order and never changes -> compute once, keep in a PriorityQueue. PREDICTION: same order; time grows ~n log n.
RESULT: identical order in all runs; 40,000 patients: ~2.3-2.6 s -> ~5-8 ms (hundreds of times faster), comparisons 1.07 million vs 800 million. Trade-offs: aging rule fixed at admission (changing W needs a rebuild), one extra long per patient. The original may be preferable for tiny n or non-linear/time-varying rules.
