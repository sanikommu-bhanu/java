---- NOTES / ANSWERS ----
A plain queue is FIFO and ignores severity. PriorityQueue with comparator (severity, then arrival counter) serves lowest severity first; the arrival counter keeps patients of equal severity in arrival order.
Printing the PriorityQueue shows the internal heap array order, not the treatment order (shown above); poll() gives the correct order and matches the hand-worked order.
Predicted add/poll O(log n); measured ns/op stays small as n grows 100x (the first row includes JIT warm-up). Starvation: a severity-5 patient waits forever while severe cases keep arriving; fair rule: score = arrival + severity x W (aging) -> Asha served 12th of 21 instead of last.
Deployment: 5,000 patients/day is a trivial load for a PriorityQueue (millions of operations/second).
ALTERNATIVES: sorted LinkedList O(n) admit, O(1) serve; array of 5 FIFO queues O(1) admit, O(1) serve (5 checks) - best when severities are few; all O(n) space.
